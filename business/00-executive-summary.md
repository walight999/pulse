## The pitch in 30 seconds

Mint (2009–2024) was the personal-finance dashboard that taught a generation to see their money. It shut down in 2024. The category went vacant.

Meanwhile, every prosumer started paying $9-$200/mo for AI tools — ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity, Lovable, v0, Replit. Average prosumer: 4-6 subscriptions, $300+/mo. By 2027: 10+ tools, $500+/mo.

Bank apps don't categorize "AI." Anthropic Console only shows Claude. ClaudeMetrics requires DevOps proxy setup. **There is no Mint for AI.**

Pulse fills that gap with three killer differentiators no competitor has:

1. **Plan ROI hero** — "Your $200 Claude returns $4,127 — Legendary tier ★★★★★"
2. **Cancellation savings tracker** — "Saved $4,300 cancelling 7 subs since starting"
3. **Cost-per-hour-of-use** — Links a subscription to its app, shows real ROI

Local-first by default. Cloud sync (Q3 2026) is opt-in + E2E encrypted. MIT licensed.

## Why this becomes a category leader
