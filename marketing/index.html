Built for power users

Three views, one source of truth — entirely on your computer.

Subscriptions

Auto-detects status: active monthly, late payment, probably yearly, or likely cancelled. Renewal reminders, calendar export, and cost-per-hour-of-use ROI for app-linked subs.

AI usage

Imports your Claude Code logs. See exact equivalent API cost vs your flat plan. Hourly heatmaps, per-project breakdowns, daily budgets with spike alerts.

Activity

Foreground app tracking with idle awareness. Auto-categorized into Dev / Browser / Communication / Entertainment. Spot what you actually use vs what you pay for.

Smart alerts

Native Windows toast notifications for upcoming renewals, AI cost spikes, and subscriptions you haven't used in months.

Local-first

Everything stored in a SQLite file on your machine. No accounts, no logins, no telemetry by default. Auto-backups + one-click restore.

Multi-currency

Display in any of 30+ currencies. Live exchange rates from ECB. Subscriptions paid in different currencies are converted automatically.
